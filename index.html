Shipping & Delivery Times

We offer Free Standard Shipping on all domestic orders over $50! Orders are packed with care in biodegradable satchels and arrive within 3–5 business days.

Express courier (1–2 days) and tracked worldwide shipping options are available during checkout.

Returns & 30-Day Hassle-Free Exchanges

Kids grow up fast! If an item doesn't fit or feel just right, exchange or return any unworn garments with original tags within 30 days.

We provide pre-paid return labels and offer instant size swaps so your little one never misses a beat.

Organic Fabric Care & Washing Guide

To keep our 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton cloud-soft and vibrant wash after wash, machine wash gentle on cold (30°C) with eco-friendly detergent.

Tumble dry low or line-dry in the breeze. No bleach or chemical fabric softeners needed!

How to Choose the Right Size for Growing Kids

All Kido garments are cut true-to-age with comfortable flex room for playtime movement. If your child is between sizes or in the middle of a growth spurt, we recommend sizing up one step for longer wear.

Check each product page for detailed length, chest, and hip measurements in cm and inches.

Are Dyes & Snaps Safe for Sensitive Baby Skin?

Yes, 100%. Every piece is certified OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class I (safe for infants) and uses non-toxic, water-based natural dyes free of heavy metals and formaldehyde.

All snaps are nickel-free and flat-stitched to prevent friction against delicate newborn skin.
