Shipping & Delivery Information

We offer standard shipping (3–5 business days) with end-to-end tracking on all domestic orders over $50. Express delivery and international shipping options are available during checkout.

Returns & 30-Day Hassle-Free Exchanges

Kids grow fast! If an item doesn't fit or feel just right, exchange or return any unworn garments with original tags within 30 days for a full refund or instant replacement size.

Organic Product Care & Washing

To keep our 100% GOTS organic cotton cloud-soft and vibrant, machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with mild detergent. Tumble dry low or line dry in the shade.

Are Dyes & Snaps Safe for Sensitive Baby Skin?

Yes, 100%. Every piece is certified OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class I (safe for infants) and uses non-toxic, water-based natural dyes free of heavy metals and formaldehyde.
